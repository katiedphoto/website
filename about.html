About me

Hi, I'm Katie.
This is my story.

I'm a Queensland-based photographer drawn to wild landscapes, star-filled skies and the simple joy of exploring somewhere new.

Photography gives me a reason to look a little closer—to notice the changing light, stay out under the stars and make time for the places that leave a lasting impression.

What brings me outside

Finding something
extraordinary
in the everyday.

You'll often find me hiking, camping or waiting for the sky to put on a show. Sometimes the reward is a photograph. Sometimes it's simply being there.

My work follows that curiosity, from the detail in a landscape to the sweep of the Milky Way. I also enjoy capturing timelapses, watching familiar places change as the light moves through them.

Through this collection and my blog, I hope to share a little of the wonder that keeps me heading outside.
